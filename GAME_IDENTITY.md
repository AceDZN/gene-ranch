# Gene Ranch — חוות הגנים · game identity

## One line
A cosy first-person creature ranch on a pastel floating island: vacuum up fluffy **Poofies (פופים)**, drop two into the breeding nest, watch a giant glowing **Punnett square** spin like a roulette wheel, an egg falls out of one cell, wobbles and pops — and the village board cheers (or groans) at what hatched.

> Naming note (26 Oct): the creatures were first called Poofies (גנומים), but the science review pointed out that Workbook uses גנום for *genome*, so in Hebrew they are now **פופים** (singular פופי). Code identifiers still say "gnoomie".

## The first 10 seconds (no reading needed)
- You stand on the ranch, net-gun in your right hand. Two fluffy Poofies hop in front of you with a **glowing ring** and a bouncing arrow over them.
- Tap/click one → it gets sucked into the net with a "fwoomp" and pops into your **creature belt** (bottom of screen).
- The **breeding nest** glows next. Tap it → the Poofie plops in. Second one → the **3D Punnett square** unfolds in the air above the nest, alleles on its edges.
- A big **egg button** pulses. Tap → the four cells light up in turn like a roulette, one cell drops an egg, wobble-wobble-POP, baby revealed.
- The **order card** (top right) shows a picture of the creature the village wants. Match it → confetti, villager cheer.

One verb: **tap** (catch, place, hatch, deliver). Walking is optional (WASD / tap the ground), never required to win.

## Camera and controls
- First person, eye height ~1.6 m, net-gun visible bottom-right.
- Drag (mouse or finger) to look around, WASD/arrows to walk, tap ground to walk there. No pointer lock (works inside the Workbook page frame).
- Everything interactive has a big glow + generous hit area. Phone works with taps only.

## World and art direction
- **Floating pastel island at golden hour:** soft peach/lilac sky, cotton-candy clouds below the island edge, warm low sun, long soft shadows.
- **Ranch set:** a round red-and-cream barn/hatchery with a weather vane, a wooden breeding nest (straw ring on a mossy platform) in front of it, a village order board on a post with a smiling villager next to it, little fences, mushrooms, a wind-mill, blossoms.
- **Poofies:** soft, rounded, Pixar-ish chubby little creatures (beach-ball body, stubby feet and arms, huge shiny eyes, tiny mouth). Fur colour, ear shape, pattern and tail are crisp and readable from 10 m:
  - ears: **upright** pointy (אוזניים זקופות) vs **floppy** droopy (אוזניים שמוטות)
  - colour: **pink** (ורוד) vs **blue** (כחול); blue Poofies that grow up in the **cold nest** turn **deep violet** (סגול) — environment, same genotype
  - pattern: **stripes** (פסים), **spots** (נקודות), or **both** (codominance)
  - transfer island: **curly vs straight tail** (זנב מסולסל / ישר) and a **glowing antenna** (מחוש זוהר)
- Egg: speckled pastel egg, wobbles, cracks with a burst of sparkles and feathers.
- **Punnett square:** a floating glass-and-brass 2×2 (or two side-by-side squares for two traits) with allele letters on glowing tiles along the top and side, each cell showing a tiny hologram of the baby phenotype + genotype letters. During a hatch the cells light in turn (roulette) and the chosen cell glows gold.

## HUD (Hebrew, RTL, Rubik)
- **Top right:** day plaque "יום 1 · אוזניים גדולות" + stars.
- **Under it:** order card(s): picture of the wanted creature, Hebrew label, check-mark when filled.
- **Top left:** round buttons (pause, sound, hint 💡) + egg counter 🥚 × 6.
- **Bottom centre:** creature belt — 4–6 round portraits (creature face with its traits), selected one raised and glowing.
- **Bottom left:** big round egg button "🥚 הטל ביצה" (pulses when ready).
- **Right edge (collapsible):** gene card of the selected creature: phenotype icons + genotype letters, unknown allele shown as **?**, "נשא" badge when deduced.
- **Hatch log:** small panel "צפוי / בפועל" (expected vs observed) — bars for each phenotype.

## Feel
Cosy, bouncy, funny. Sounds: vacuum whoosh, squeaky "pip!" when a Poofie lands, egg wobble rattles, pop + choir "aaah", villager cheers. Wrong breeding is never a red X: a surprising baby, a villager sweat-drop and a short worked explanation.

## Not this
Not a worksheet with a 3D background, not a courier loop, not robots/industrial toys. The camera is first person on a living ranch, the creatures are the stars.
